This is the home page. It, and everything else on this site, is written as
a plain markdown file under `content/`.

Two things make this different from a normal blog:

1. **Wikilinks.** Write [[Hello, World]] and it resolves to the page whose
   title, slug, or alias matches -- no need to remember the exact path.
   Hover it and you'll get a preview, gwern.net-style.
2. **Backlinks.** Every page automatically lists what links to it, at the
   bottom, under "Linked from" -- see [[On Note-Taking]] for an example.

See [all pages](/all/) or [tags](/tags/) to browse, or link directly to a
file, e.g. [Hello, World](essays/hello-world.md).
