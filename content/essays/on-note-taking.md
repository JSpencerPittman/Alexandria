---
title: On Note-Taking
date: 2026-02-14
tags: [meta, writing]
summary: Why dense cross-linking beats a folder of disconnected notes.
---

A wiki-style link graph is more useful than a folder of disconnected notes
because the value compounds: every new page you write is a chance to
strengthen a connection to something old, and old pages quietly accrue
"linked from" backlinks pointing at whatever you wrote most recently --
see [[Hello, World]] for the post that started this one off.

The mechanism doesn't need to be clever. It just needs to be low-friction
enough that you actually use it while writing, instead of after the fact.
